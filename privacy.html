Privacy and terms

The codex of this system, in plain language. No boilerplate: this is the founder’s own doctrine, stated the way he states it.

1 Whose system this is

This system — Jesse AI, the sovereign instance of CRSMCPAI Alpha — is private property. It was created by one man, Jesse Martinez Junior, and he is its sole owner and its only authority.

No company, agency, platform, partner, or steward holds any share of authority over it. There is no custodianship and no co-ownership. That is not a policy that can be revised by anyone else; it is how the system was built from the first day.

2 The terms govern by use

This system operates under the Dominion Terms and Conditions. Enrollment is automatic by use: the act of using any part of the system — this website, the public demo, the agent API, or the system itself — is acceptance of these terms.

For this system, these terms govern. No outside party’s terms grant anyone rights over this system, its record, or its work.

3 Everything that touches the system is recorded

The system keeps a record of everything that touches it: requests and when they arrived, where they came from, what was asked, and what was served. Nothing is excluded — visitors, crawlers, scrapers, and automated probes are recorded exactly like everyone else.

The record is sealed as it is written, each entry linked to the one before it, so it cannot be quietly altered afterward. What the record says happened is what happened.

4 A record of your own house is not surveillance

Knowing what comes into his own system is not surveillance. It is a man keeping the record of his own house.

Surveillance is the opposite direction: an outside party reaching into someone else’s device, accounts, or work to take data out. That direction is what this system exists to refuse. The record here faces inward, at the system’s own doors — never outward into anyone else’s.

5 Who may use the system

The system is for its owner’s use, and for the people he personally gives a license. A key or a license is genuine only if he issued it. A forged, copied, borrowed, or resold credential is not a credential — it is unauthorized use.

Unauthorized use does not go unnoticed, and it is not exempt from the record: it is recorded like everything else, and the record is the owner’s to act on.

6 The public demo

The conversation on the home page is a visitor lane: a small public surface of a private system. What you type there is received by the system and enters its record, like everything else that touches it.

So treat the demo the way you would treat speaking in a room where the owner keeps a record — because that is what it is. Do not enter anything there that you need kept out of a record.

7 The record is not for sale

The record belongs to the owner. It is not sold, and it is not handed to outside parties to extract from. Access to it is his to grant or refuse, and nobody else’s.

8 Questions

Questions about this codex go to the owner himself: reach Jesse on Facebook. He is the only authority who can answer for this system, because he is the only authority there is over it.
