2 Why the first answer can be slow

The live system runs on a free hosting tier. It sleeps after about fifteen quiet minutes, and waking takes about a minute. A slow first answer is the system waking up, not the system failing — and a check from this page can itself be the knock that wakes it. If a check below comes back not checked, wait about a minute and press Check again.
